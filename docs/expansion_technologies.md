# Prophecy of Kings and Codex technologies

The research catalog includes the base game, Prophecy of Kings, and the latest replacement printings of Magen Defense Grid and X-89 Bacterial Weapon from Codex 4.5. Magen Defense Grid uses `md`; X-89 Bacterial Weapon uses `x89c4`. Earlier base and Omega printings are retained in the bundled data for provenance but are not offered during research. Faction technologies for the seven Prophecy of Kings factions and the Council Keleres are included.

The generic Prophecy of Kings cards added to the catalog are Dark Energy Tap, Sling Relay, Psychoarchaeology, Bio-Stims, AI Development Algorithm, Self-Assembly Routines, Scanlink Drone Network, and Predictive Intelligence. The technology action picker supports Sling Relay, Psychoarchaeology, Bio-Stims, and Predictive Intelligence. Predictive Intelligence presents complete command-pool allocations while preserving Fleet Supply. AI Development Algorithm's ready prerequisite skip and Psychoarchaeology's technology-specialty exception, including specialties from exploration attachments, are exposed to the research payment flow.

Technology card scans and rules text come from the pinned AsyncTI4 resource data. The official references are the [Prophecy of Kings rules](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/c4/b9/c4b94e1e-d392-4ef8-a305-a37bb5660be3/ti10_rulebook_web.pdf), [Codex I](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/94/c5/94c59830-533e-47aa-a708-25634575161b/twilight_codex_v1-compressed.pdf), and [Codex 4.5 technology cards](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/e9/8f/e98f4722-2f45-4328-9119-56508bf1c94b/codex_45_technology_card_updates.pdf).
