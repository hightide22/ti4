# Combat and movement action-card shortlist

The board currently uses a focused 18-card starter deck built from six supported card types. Politics draws up to two cards for its owner; each other player can spend a Strategy token to draw up to two. Each completed status phase also draws one card for every player. The hand limit is seven; drawing stops at the limit for now.

The initial playable set is **Flank Speed**, **Morale Boost**, **Fighter Prototype**, **Shields Holding**, **Emergency Repairs**, and **Skilled Retreat**. The card window in the game keeps other cards dim and marks a playable card with **PLAY NOW**. The list below contains only base-game cards selected for later implementation.

| Card | Timing | Short effect | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bunker | Start of invasion | Makes bombardment less effective against your planets | Planned |
| Courageous to the End | After one of your ships is destroyed in space combat | Roll to destroy opposing ships | Planned |
| Direct Hit | After an enemy ship uses Sustain Damage against your hit | Destroy that ship | Planned |
| Disable | Start of invasion against opposing PDS | Turns off Planetary Shield and Space Cannon for that invasion | Planned |
| Emergency Repairs | Start or end of a combat round | Repair your damaged Sustain Damage units in the active system | Implemented |
| Experimental Battlestation | After ships move into the active system | Fire a nearby Space Dock's Space Cannon | Planned |
| Fighter Prototype | Start of first space-combat round | +2 to your fighters' combat rolls this round | Implemented |
| Fire Team | After ground-combat rolls | Reroll any number of your dice | Planned |
| Flank Speed | After system activation | +1 movement for each of your ships this tactical action | Implemented |
| In the Silence of Space | After system activation | Let ships in a chosen system move through enemy-occupied systems | Planned |
| Intercept | After an opponent announces a retreat | Prevent that retreat this round | Planned |
| Maneuvering Jets | Before assigning Space Cannon hits | Cancel one hit | Planned |
| Morale Boost | Start of a combat round | +1 to all your combat rolls this round | Implemented |
| Salvage | After winning a space combat | Take the opponent's commodities | Planned |
| Shields Holding | Before assigning hits to your ships in space combat | Cancel up to two hits | Implemented |
| Skilled Retreat | Start of a space-combat round | Retreat all your ships and end combat in a draw | Implemented |
| Upgrade | After activation when your ships are in the system | Replace a cruiser with a dreadnought | Planned |
| War Effort | Component action | Place a cruiser in a system containing your ship | Planned |
| Fighter Conscription | Component action | Place fighters in eligible systems | Planned |

This is a shortlist, not a complete deck list. Candidate names were checked against the `base` source records in the pinned AsyncTI4 action-card data.

## Card artwork

Face images for all 18 shortlisted base-game cards are included in `assets/action_cards/`. The six implemented cards use their scans in the hand; the remaining images are reserved for later implementation. The files were taken from the English action-card textures in the public [TI4-TTPG repository](https://github.com/TI4-Online/TI4-TTPG/tree/main/prebuild/Textures/en/card/action). Twilight Imperium card artwork remains the property of its original rights holder.
