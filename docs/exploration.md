# Exploration and secret-objective draws

Planetary and frontier exploration use the Prophecy of Kings decks plus the six Codex III frontier cards in the bundled resource data. Exploration resolves each card once and keeps attachments, fragments, and relics in their appropriate zones.

**Freelancers** opens a one-unit production offer with unit, destination, and payment controls. `ExplorationController.produce_freelancers(player, movement, kind, planet_id=None, resource_planets=(), influence_planets=(), trade_goods=0)` validates the choice before exhausting planets or placing the unit. Resource and influence planet selections can be mixed, but each ready planet can pay only once. This effect does not use a unit's **PRODUCTION** ability, so Sarween Tools and AI Development Algorithm do not reduce its cost. Pass `kind=None` to decline. After placement, the normal capacity and fleet checks run before exploration resumes. A pending offer blocks a new exploration draw.

**Derelict Vessel** draws from a separate 40-card base-game and Prophecy of Kings secret-objective deck into the owner's private hand. `SecretObjectiveController.deal_starting_hands()` deals one starting secret to every player. The limit of three counts scored and unscored secrets together. A draw beyond that limit opens an owner-only choice of an unscored card for `return_unscored(faction, alias)`; the card is returned to the deck and the deck is shuffled. Exploration pauses until the excess card is returned. General objective scoring is not yet implemented. See the [official Prophecy of Kings Living Rules Reference](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/51/55/51552c7f-c05c-445b-84bf-4b073456d008/ti10_pok_living_rules_reference_20_web.pdf) for the secret-objective limit and production timing.
